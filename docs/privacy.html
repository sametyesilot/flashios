Lumora Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Summary

Lumora does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data we collect

None. The app only controls your device's flashlight (torch). It does not access your camera image, photos, location, contacts, microphone, or any other personal information.

Purchases

Lumora Pro subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your selected theme and a free-use counter locally on your device (UserDefaults and Keychain). This data never leaves your device.

Children

Lumora does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.
