Lumora Support

How do I cancel Lumora Pro?

Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Lumora → Cancel Subscription.

I already subscribed but Pro isn't active.

Open the Pro screen and tap “Restore Purchases” while signed in with the same Apple ID.

The flashlight doesn't turn on.

Close the Camera app, make sure Low Power Mode or high device temperature isn't limiting the flash, then try again.
