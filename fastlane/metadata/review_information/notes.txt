Lumora is a single-screen flashlight app.

How to test:
1. Tap the large power button: the flashlight turns on (free).
2. Tap again: the flashlight turns off. Turning the light off is always free.
3. Tap again to turn it on: the Lumora Pro subscription screen appears. It can be closed with the X button at any time.

Lumora Pro (monthly auto-renewable subscription) unlocks unlimited flashlight use and 4 additional premium themes (theme dots at the bottom). Restore Purchases, Terms of Use and Privacy Policy are on the subscription screen.

The app collects no data, needs no account and does not request camera permission (it only controls the torch).
The flashlight requires a device with a rear LED flash (UIRequiredDeviceCapabilities: camera-flash).
